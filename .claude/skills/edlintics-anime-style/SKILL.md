# Edlintics anime style

Reference studied: "This is…THE FINAL TEST | Duolingo Anime - All episodes" (Duolingo's YouTube channel, 7:03, https://youtu.be/tlotyLGia5I), read as 36 frames on 20 Nov 2026. It is a comedy shonen parody: flat 2D cel animation, loud reactions, a new background mood for every emotion.

## 1. What the style is (from the frames)

| Element | What the reference does | Our version |
|---|---|---|
| Line | Clean dark outline of even weight (dark brown or navy, not pure black), thicker on the silhouette, thin inside | Navy #111A2C outline, thick silhouette |
| Colour | Flat fills, very saturated; ONE hard-edged shadow tone per shape, no gradients on characters | Same; Edlintics blue #467FE3 is the lead colour in every frame |
| Light | Rim light and glow come from flat shapes with hard edges, plus white sparkle dots | Same |
| Eyes | The emotion lives in the eyes: large glossy eyes with two or three white highlights and a coloured iris in close-ups; small dots in wide shots; half-lidded for smug; blank white circles for shock or rage | Ledger keeps round dark eyes with one highlight in normal shots; in close-ups they grow and get two highlights; the other states below |
| Mouth | Tiny in calm shots, huge open shape with a tongue for shouting or joy | Same, inside Ledger's cover |
| Reaction marks | Sweat drop, blush hatch lines, tear streams, anger vein, sparkles, shock lines, a glint on glasses | Same marks, drawn in navy or blue |
| Backgrounds | Change with the emotion: orange-red speed lines for rage, pink sparkles and bubbles for cute, dark vignette for menace, painted blue sky with clouds for calm, split diagonal panels for a reaction cut-in | Same idea, palette pulled towards blue, cream and one accent per scene |
| Camera | Extreme close-up on eyes, low angle for power, wide establishing shot with tiny figures, top-down fisheye room | Same |
| Text | Title cards in bold 3D-styled lettering; subtitles in yellow | Our titles in Rubik bold; captions stay in the bottom half, full sentences |
| Proportions | Chibi-like: big heads, small bodies, simple hands | Ledger is already chibi: square book body about 85% of his height, stub legs about one tenth of the body, short thin arms |

## 2. Ledger's reaction library

Ledger's face is on his cover: two eyes, two brow lines, one mouth, the blue bookmark, the page lines above the eyes. Reactions change ONLY those plus the marks around him. His body stays a square book.

| Reaction | Eyes | Brows | Mouth | Marks | Background |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bored / sigh | Half-lidded, looking down | Flat, low | Small flat line, a puff of breath | One sweat drop | Grey-blue, dim, vertical gloom lines |
| Curious | Round, one highlight, looking to the side | One raised | Small "o" | Question mark | Calm blue sky with clouds |
| Shock | Blank white circles with a thin outline | High, out of the cover line | Large open rectangle | Shock lines, bookmark stands straight up | White flash, radial lines |
| Smug / proud | Half-lidded with a curve under | One raised | Wide closed smirk | Small glint star | Dark blue vignette |
| Joy / cheer | Closed happy arcs or huge sparkly eyes with three highlights | Up | Huge open smile with tongue | Sparkles, blush hatch lines | Yellow-cream radial burst, confetti |
| Determined | Sharp, narrowed | Angled down to the centre | Tight line | Speed lines behind, bookmark flaps back | Blue and white speed lines |
| Panic | Small pupils, wide whites | Wavy | Wavy open mouth | Three sweat drops, shaking outline | Orange-red speed lines |
| Moved / grateful | Big glossy eyes, tear at the corner | Tilted up at the centre | Small wobbling smile | Bubbles and sparkles | Pink-blue soft sparkle field |
| Aha | Wide with star highlights | Up | Open smile | Light-bulb glint, exclamation mark | Bright blue burst |

Rule: one reaction per frame, and it must be readable with the sound off.

## 2b. Body rules: short limbs, movement instead of reach, angles (Hoang, 20 Nov 2026)

- **Arms and legs never stretch.** Arms are short and thin with small round hands that reach no further than the edge of the book; legs are stubs. A frame where an arm crosses half the screen to touch something fails.
- **He moves to the thing.** To reach, drag or place something he walks to it, climbs a short set of steps or a ladder, hops onto a stool, jumps, pushes with his whole body, or carries the object against his cover with both small hands and drops it in place. "Drag and drop" is: pick up, carry, walk or climb, drop. Write that movement into the scene line and the prompt ("he stands on the third step of a small staircase holding the block in both hands").
- **Scale the props to him.** Blocks, sliders and dots are about the size of his hands or body, low on the screen, with steps or platforms up to anything higher.
- **Angles.** He is a book with thickness (about a quarter of his width): the spine on his right-hand side, page edges on the other, a plain back cover. Use the turnaround sheet (front, 3/4 front, side, 3/4 back, back) and pick the angle that fits the action: 3/4 for walking and carrying, side for climbing and pushing, back when he looks at a big screen, front for talking to the viewer. The face stays on the front cover only. All 2D: thickness is drawn with flat shapes and one shadow tone.

## 4. Match checklist (look at the frame at full size)

1. Flat cel shading: one shadow tone, hard edge. Fail if soft gradients, 3D or painted realism.
2. Outline: even dark line, thicker silhouette.
3. Ledger on model: square book, stub legs, short arms that do not stretch past his body, bookmark, tie, suit bottom, face on the cover; turned views show thickness.
4. The reaction reads in under a second and uses the anime marks from the table.
5. The background carries the emotion and is not a neutral room unless the row says so.
6. Blue is the lead colour; at most one strong accent.
7. Nothing of the reference's own world: no green owl, none of its human cast, no flame over heads, no Japanese title card copied.
8. Any text is a full sentence, spelled right, with no full stop at the end.
Fix once by regenerating with the failing point stated first in the prompt; a second failure goes to Hoang with the frame.

## 5. Use

- Storyboard frames: fill the template per scene, generate, check, save the full-size file, upload to the review doc.
- Flat (Duolingo spot) style is a different look: strictly flat vector, no shadow tone, minimal background. Do not mix the two in one video.
- Animated scenes later: the same reaction rows drive the pose and the background swap on each beat.
