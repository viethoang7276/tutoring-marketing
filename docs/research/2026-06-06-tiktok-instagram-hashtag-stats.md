| `#businessschool` | **43.9 K** | Mid | Campus life, MBA, applications, day-in-the-life. |
| `#corporatefinance` | **5.6 K** | Mid (wrong intent) | **Careers** — IB vs FP&A, recruiting season, salaries. Not exam concepts. |
| `#financialaccounting` | **4.0 K** | **Highest (exact)** | **Concept explainers** — the exact lane. See §2. |

### The single most important number here
`#financialaccounting` has **~4,036 videos** vs `#studytok`'s **4.2 million** — a **~1,000× gap**. The exact-concept lane is nearly empty. That is the opportunity and the trap in one number:
- **Opportunity:** almost no competition for "the person who explains *this exam concept* well." Easy to become the definitive result.
- **Trap:** 4K videos = thin native browse traffic on that tag alone. You won't get *discovered* sitting only in the concept tag. **Reach comes from stacking a big tag (#studytok / #accountingstudent) on top of the precise one.** (Hashtag-stack recommendation in §4.)

## 2. The competitive content pattern (who owns the concept lane, and how)

`#financialaccounting` and `#corporatefinance` are dominated by a recognisable archetype worth studying:

- **`vinnyhale`** — the clear leader. Dozens of sub-60s **concept-truth one-liners**, each one idea + one visual:
  - "Cash is real, revenue is opinion."
  - "Deferred revenue is about obligation, not just cash."
  - "Deferred taxes = timing differences between book and tax."
  - "Depreciation is the sneakiest accounting trick — here's why it matters."
  - "Materiality is about what actually impacts decisions."
  - **His angle is finance-*professional* / CPA / careers — not "pass your university exam."**
- **`brianferoldi`** — "3 Financial Statements Explained" (explainer carousel/video style).
- **`jackzzheng`** — "Your entire accounting education in 90 seconds" (compression hook).
- **`gracedziedzic`** — empathetic "accounting is hard, you're not alone" + one hack. (Bridges concept ↔ the struggle genre.)
- **`theaccountingrapper`, `productivevan`** — "how to pass intro to financial accounting", "first vs last accounting class".

**The open gap (confirms yesterday's desk research):** nobody owns **undergraduate-exam-specific, worked-on-screen problems** in a calm voice. The leaders teach *concepts in the abstract* for a *professional/US-CPA* audience. None do "here's the kind of problem your Year-1 Financial Accounting midterm will actually ask, solved live, you-drive-style." That is exactly our product's format.

### Two platform signals worth noting
- TikTok is pushing **`#creatorsearchinsights`** and **`#tiktoklearningcampaign`** — it's actively rewarding **search-optimised educational** content. TikTok is behaving like a search engine; **concept-named videos get found on intent**, not just served on the For You page.
- The **relatable-struggle genre** (`#accountingstudent`) and the **concept-truth genre** (`#financialaccounting`) are currently *separate*. The winning recipe is to fuse them: a struggle/empathy **hook** ("the consolidation question everyone loses marks on") wrapped around a concept-truth **payload**, delivered in our **worked-example-on-screen** format. Nobody is doing all three together.

## 3. Instagram findings (qualitative — counts are gone)

- **Instagram no longer publishes hashtag post counts.** `/explore/tags/financialaccounting/` redirects to a keyword-search grid with **no number** shown. So IG can't be sized quantitatively the way TikTok can — a real limitation for competitive benchmarking.
- The niche grid is a **mix of carousels and Reels** (confirmed visually — carousel and play-button icons on tiles). Reels is the video surface that matters; static carousels (explainer slides) are the secondary format here.
- **Implication:** treat Instagram as a **repurpose target, not a discovery-measurement tool.** Reels mechanics from yesterday's research still apply (watch-time, likes/reach, **sends/reach** — "send to your study group" is the lever). Don't expect to *measure* niche size on IG; measure on TikTok, repurpose to IG/Reels and YouTube Shorts.

## 4. Recommended hashtag strategy (TikTok / Reels)

Stack **one tag from each tier** per post — reach + topic + intent + platform:

| Tier | Purpose | Tags to rotate |
|---|---|---|
| **Reach** (big) | Get served beyond the niche | `#studytok` · `#accountingstudent` |
| **Topic** (mid) | Land in the right audience | `#accounting` · `#businessschool` · `#financemajor` |
| **Intent** (exact) | Win the concept search | `#financialaccounting` · the specific concept (`#taccounts`, `#cashflowstatement`, `#consolidation`, `#deferredtax`) |
| **Search/platform** | Ride TikTok's search push | `#creatorsearchinsights` · `#fyp` · `#learnontiktok` |

Avoid leaning on `#fintok` / `#corporatefinance` for reach — high follower counts there, **wrong buying intent** (money influencers / job-seekers, not exam-panicked undergrads).

---

## 5. How this updates the strategy

1. **Confirms "don't chase fintok."** `#fintok` (161K) and `#corporatefinance` (5.6K) are sized fine but are personal-finance / careers — not our buyer. The data backs the §2 call from yesterday.
2. **Confirms the format gap is real and ownable.** The concept lane (`#financialaccounting`, 4K) is uncrowded and its leader (`vinnyhale`) aims at professionals, leaving the undergrad-exam angle open.
3. **Sharpens the hook recipe:** struggle-hook + concept-truth payload + worked-on-screen format — three things that currently live in separate videos by separate creators.
4. **Sets the discovery mechanic:** stack a big reach tag with the exact concept tag; you will *not* be found sitting only in the 4K concept tag.
5. **Right-sizes Instagram:** repurpose target, not a measurement or discovery-led channel. Lead measurement on TikTok and search on YouTube.

## Method & caveats

- **Source:** live reads of TikTok hashtag pages (counts are TikTok's own, as displayed 2026-06-06) + Instagram explore/tag pages, via a logged-in Chrome session. **I only viewed public hashtag pages — no posting, messaging, following, or any action on the logged-in TikTok (`@`)/Instagram (`_hoang___`) accounts.**
- **TikTok counts** fluctuate and are approximate/rounded by TikTok; treat as order-of-magnitude, not precise.
- **`#studywithme`** returned "page not available" on the attempt (intermittent TikTok gating), so it's omitted.
- **Instagram counts are unavailable by design** — Meta removed public hashtag counts; the §3 findings are qualitative.
- **Content-pattern reads** are from the first ~25–30 videos surfaced per tag (TikTok's own ranking), not an exhaustive census. Non-English content (esp. Vietnamese, Spanish) is heavily present on the broad tags and dilutes reach for an English-only, EU-targeted account — a reason to lean on the precise tags + search intent rather than raw big-tag volume.
