Method: I read these from the hub on 5 Nov: `slots` from 8 Oct onward, `perf/latest` v26 (window to 4 Nov), `trends`, `requests`, `reviews` and `concepts`, plus the repo concept mirror. I added the 5 Nov trend scan (`trends/2026-11-05-scan-1..13`, `requests/2026-11-05-trend-1..5`). The research used public WebSearch and WebFetch only, with no Chrome and no logins. Every planned post went through a scripted registry check (`check.py`, run against the plan and the mirror):
- the same concept as a main idea within 90 days;
- a related concept within 30 days;
- a how-to industry within 30 days;
- more than 2 main posts per theme in any 7-day window;
- list-reel supporting terms reused within 30 days.
