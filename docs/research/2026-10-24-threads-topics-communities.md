# Threads topics and communities for Edlintics (24 Oct 2026)

**Method.** This combines web research with a read-only look at threads.com, logged in as @edlintics. Nothing was posted, liked, followed or joined.

**Caveats.**
- Counts are copied as shown on 24 Oct 2026.
- Search results are personalised, and our feed leans to Vietnam.
- "Top" posts are a sample, not totals.

## 1. Headline finding: every Edlintics thread is tagged "edlintics"

All 6 Edlintics threads visible on our profile carry the topic tag **edlintics**, replies included. This covers the accounting video, the returns puzzle, the sequencing puzzle and their first comments.

- **Why this costs reach:** the topic is how Threads groups posts and suggests them to interested people. "edlintics" is a topic only we use, so the tag does no distribution work.
- **Likely cause:** the scheduler types the first hashtag of the caption (`#edlintics`) into the topic chip.
- **The fix:** choose the topic per post (section 5), and remove `#edlintics` from the Threads text.

## 2. How topics and communities work (2026)

**Topic tags**
- One per post.
- Can be several words with spaces.
- Typing `#` opens suggestions, ordered by how many threads use each one.
- Tapping a tag opens a feed of posts that share it.
- Meta says tags put a post alongside others on the same topic. Third-party guides add that recommendations use the tag, but Meta has not documented this.
- A guide reports the tag cannot be changed after posting.

Sources:
- [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/threads-hashtags-topic-tags-435595)
- [MarTech](https://martech.org/marketers-can-now-use-topic-tags-on-threads-to-boost-engagement/)
- [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2023/12/07/threads-adds-tags-a-simplified-version-of-hashtags/)
- [SocialNewsDesk](https://www.socialnewsdesk.com/blog/threads-topic-tagging-how-to-use-this-brand-new-feature)

**Communities**
- Launched as a worldwide beta in October 2025, with more than 100 interest communities. This grew to more than 200 by December 2025, with flair and "champion" badges, and later left beta with a Communities Hub.
- **How they work:** you join a community, and members get a dedicated feed. The community name works like a topic: posting with that topic posts into the community. Each community has its own like emoji.
- **Naming:** communities are named "X Threads", for example Book Threads and Tech Threads.

Sources:
- [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/introducing-threads-communities-find-your-people/)
- [TechCrunch, Oct 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/02/threads-takes-on-x-with-new-communities-feature)
- [TechCrunch, Dec 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/15/threads-adds-new-communities-tests-badges-for-highly-engaged-members)
- [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/10/02/threads-announces-topic-based-communities/)

**Brand accounts:** Threads accounts are profiles, so @edlintics can technically join and post. Joining is a visible account action, so it needs Hoang's approval. I found no rule against brands, but self-promotion communities moderate heavily (section 6).

**Caption rules for Threads**
- Put the question in the first line.
- Keep it under about 300 characters before the options.
- Write no hashtags in the text; set the topic chip instead.

## 6. Risks

- **Spam-heavy community:** Business Threads is mostly "drop your link" posts, and a brand puzzle there reads as promotion.
- **Locked tag:** the topic tag can't be edited after posting, according to third-party guides. It must be right at scheduling time.
- **Local skew:** our search results lean to Vietnam, so Vietnamese posts dominate some topics such as Data Science and CPA. The English audience may look different.
- **Visible footprint:** joining a community shows membership on our profile. That's a brand decision for Hoang.
- **Sample limits:** engagement numbers here come from a handful of top posts, not averages.
