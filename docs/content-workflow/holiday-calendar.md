# Holiday calendar (added 31 Oct 2026, Hoang)

Hoang's rule (31 Oct 2026): note every holiday and make the content fit it. Christmas gets its own content run starting 1–2 weeks before 25 Dec.

How the planner uses this file:
- Every Thursday plan checks this table for holidays in the next 3 weeks (step 4a of the Thursday run in `weekly-content-process.md`).
- **Content window:** the days before a holiday when themed posts go out. Most holidays get 1–3 posts on the day or the day before. Big ones (Christmas, New Year, Black Friday, Lunar New Year) get a run of posts across the whole window.
- **Plan by:** the Thursday plan that has to include the posts, so they can be built and approved in time.
- **Themed post:** a normal Edlintics format (puzzle, reel, five concepts, comic, carousel) whose story or numbers come from the holiday. It must still teach one business concept. Recent market or price news makes it stronger; check every number, in USD.
- **Ledger costumes:** Ledger keeps his standard suit and gets one costume piece on top (Halloween 2026: Dracula cape and fangs, `dracula` outfit in `scene_lib.py`). Add new pieces the same way, for example a Santa hat or a scarf.
- **Holiday music:** only from `docs/research/2026-10-31-christmas-music.md` (copyright-safe sources), at Canva volume 8 under the voice.
- **Religious or national days:** respectful and educational only, with no jokes about the faith or the country.
- **Moving dates:** dates marked "(est.)" depend on moon sighting or official calendars. Check them again 4 weeks before.

## Christmas 2026

- **Window:** 11 to 24 Dec, with a warm post on 25 Dec. About 2 themed posts a day inside normal slots, not extra posts.
- **Plan:** the Thursday plan of 3 Dec drafts the whole run (weeks 50 to 52). The 10 Dec and 17 Dec plans refresh it with the latest news.
- **Approval:** the first Christmas posts need approval by 9 Dec.
- **Formats:**
  - "A day in the life" reels: Santa's CFO, an elf in inventory, a toy-shop owner in December.
  - Puzzles: gift budgets, a Christmas-tree price spike, shipping cut-off dates, cash vs a gift card.
  - A "5 Christmas business concepts" video: seasonality, peak inventory, gift-card breakage, returns reserve, holiday working capital.
  - Comics: the office secret Santa budget.
- **News hooks to check at the time, all in USD:**
  - the NRF holiday spending forecast (published in November);
  - Christmas tree, turkey and toy prices;
  - shipping and tariff news;
  - Adobe online holiday sales data.
- **Ledger:** standard suit plus a Santa hat or scarf (add an outfit like `dracula`).
- **Music:** copyright-safe Christmas tracks only, from `docs/research/2026-10-31-christmas-music.md`. Public-domain melodies (Jingle Bells, Silent Night, Deck the Halls, The Nutcracker) are fine only in a recording we have the rights to, such as the Canva library or a public-domain recording. Never use modern songs or covers of them, for example "All I Want for Christmas Is You".
