## Summary
Students search specific exam concepts ("how to do an indirect cash-flow statement") on Google and
YouTube when they are stuck. We have no free, indexable concept surface to capture that intent and
warm the visitor before the course page. The existing `/blog` is general, not concept-per-page.

## Evidence (lead with this)
- YouTube/Google are functioning as revision search engines; concept-named content captures
  high-intent students at the moment of need (research, 2026-06-05 report).
- The exact-concept content lane is nearly empty (TikTok `#financialaccounting` ≈ 4K videos vs
  `#studytok` 4.2M) — low competition to rank for named concepts.
- Course **landing** pages convert far higher (≈13–18% median) than cold sales pages (≈2–5%);
  a pre-warmed visitor is worth materially more (conversion-benchmark research).

## Hypothesis
We believe that **a free, indexable concept page per flagship topic** (with a soft CTA to the
matching €99 course) for **an exam-anxious undergraduate searching that concept** will cause
**qualified course-page visits and their conversion** to rise, because the visitor arrives already
helped and already trusting, at the moment of peak intent.

## Job to be done (problem, not solution)
"When I am stuck on a specific exam concept the night before, I want a clear, trustworthy page that
actually solves it, so that I can pass — and if it is good, I will look at what else this person offers."

## Proposed requirements (CTO owns the final design)
- The system shall serve a public, indexable page per flagship concept (T-accounts, cash-flow
  statement, consolidation, deferred tax, CVP), with correct SEO metadata and structured data.
- The system shall present a worked example and a soft, non-hype CTA to the relevant course.
- The system shall keep these pages topic-led and independence-compliant (no university in the
  headline; standard disclaimer present).

## Success signal (how marketing will know it worked)
Organic impressions/clicks on named-concept queries climb through the autumn; click-through from a
concept page to its course page is measurable and rises over the season.

## Out-of-scope check
Does not touch any locked out-of-scope item (no AI student tutor, gamification, subscription,
certificate, multi-tutor). Adjacent-but-distinct from `/blog`, which exists but is not concept-paged.
