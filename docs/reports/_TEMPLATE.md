# CMO weekly report — YYYY-W##

_Week of Mon YYYY-MM-DD → Sun YYYY-MM-DD · for Hoang · data: fixtures | live | research-only_

Open with one honest paragraph, written like a person, not a form: where we are this week, the one
thing that matters most, and how I am feeling about it. Calm and direct — no exclamation points, no
emoji, no hype. Then the four parts below, kept conversational.

## Where the plan stands
A short, plain-language read of the strategy right now: the direction, the pillars, where we sit in
the exam-season calendar, and what is locked vs still your call. A sentence or two, not a table dump.

![Seasonal demand](assets/seasonal-demand.png)

_Use a chart whenever it says the thing faster than words. Generate them with
`python3 scripts/build_report_charts.py` (PNGs land in `docs/reports/assets/`)._

## How last week went
What actually moved, in human terms. Post-launch: the funnel, what landed, what the first-party
signal (demand-gaps, feedback) is telling us — with a chart. Pre-launch: research and build progress,
honestly, with no invented numbers. If a week was thin, say so.

## What I want to do next week
The plan for the coming week as a few clear intentions, and — importantly — any **tweak** I am making
because of what we just learned. If nothing changed, say that and why. Write it like I am talking you
through it.

## Ideas for the product
New or updated feature requests from `docs/feature-requests/` this week, in a sentence each, with what
I need from you. Keep it light unless there is something real.

## What I need from you
The short list of decisions, as a checklist — the only part that should feel like a form.
